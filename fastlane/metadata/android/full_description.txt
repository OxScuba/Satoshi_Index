Satoshi Index lets you track the evolution of everyday product prices in both euros (€) and bitcoins (₿), displayed in satoshis.
A new way to understand inflation and purchasing power over time, with:

- Live bitcoin price updated automatically (CoinGecko)
- Interactive charts for each product (baguette, coffee, gasoline, real estate, etc.)
- Detailed historical tables with quarterly prices
- Educational display for satoshi to bitcoin conversion
- Bitcoin donation page (QR code)
- Official sources (INSEE, CoinGecko)
- Dark mode and customizable display settings

Open-source application – download the APK or contribute freely on GitHub!
